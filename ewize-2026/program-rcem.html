Recertification for Energy Managers

The Recertification Training for Energy Managers provides accredited Energy Managers with the latest knowledge, technical updates, and advanced skills required to maintain their certification with the Department of Energy. This two-day program keeps participants aligned with current policies, regulatory issuances, and emerging technologies in energy efficiency and conservation.

DOE registration

DOE-RTI-RCEM-250711511

Duration

2 days

Who it is for

Accredited Energy Managers renewing DOE certification

P-04Curriculum

Key topics
covered.

Delivered by practising energy professionals and certified instructors. Sessions combine classroom work with hands-on use of audit instrumentation.

01
EEC Act Updates
Updates on the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act and related DOE issuances.
02
Level 2 Energy Audit
Introduction to detailed audit methodology.
03
Energy Conserving Design for Buildings
Introduction to conserving design principles.
04
Advanced Mechanical Systems
Current practice in mechanical system efficiency.
05
Advanced Electrical Systems
Current practice in electrical system efficiency.
06
Advanced Building Envelope
Envelope performance and improvement.
07
Sustainability, Environmental & Water Systems Design
Integrated resource management.
08
IoT and Data Analytics for Energy Efficiency
Instrumentation and analysis for continuous improvement.
