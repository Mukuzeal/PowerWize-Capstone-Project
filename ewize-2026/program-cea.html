Certified Energy Auditor

The Certified Energy Auditor program equips professionals with the expertise to conduct energy audits, identify energy-saving opportunities, and ensure compliance with energy efficiency regulations. Successful completion enables participants to apply for certification with the Department of Energy as a Certified Energy Auditor.

DOE registration

DOE-RTI-CEA-250400113

Duration

7 days across two blocks

Who it is for

Practising auditors, consulting engineers, ESCO technical staff

P-02Curriculum

Key topics
covered.

Delivered by practising energy professionals and certified instructors. Sessions combine classroom work with hands-on use of audit instrumentation.

01
ISO 50001 — Energy Management System
Basic awareness.
02
ISO 50002 — Energy Audit Standards
Basic awareness.
03
Energy Laws & Regulations
RA 11285, EPIRA, the Renewable Energy Act, and DOE circulars.
04
Operation & Maintenance of Audit Tools
Power meters, thermal cameras, leak detectors, and related instruments.
05
Energy Audit Planning & Execution
Site inspections, data collection, and reporting.
06
Technical Competencies
Building envelope performance; pump, steam and compressed air optimisation; electrical and mechanical systems; lighting system efficiency.
