Recertification of Energy Auditors

The Recertification Training for Energy Auditors is a two-day advanced program designed to support accredited Energy Auditors in renewing their certification with the Department of Energy. It keeps participants current on policies, technical methodologies, and industry best practice in energy auditing and energy efficiency project evaluation.

DOE registration

DOE-RTI-RCEA-250711321

Duration

2 days

Who it is for

Accredited Energy Auditors renewing DOE certification

P-05Curriculum

Key topics
covered.

Delivered by practising energy professionals and certified instructors. Sessions combine classroom work with hands-on use of audit instrumentation.

01
EEC Act Updates
Updates on the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act and related DOE issuances.
02
Level 2 Energy Audit
Applied detailed audit methodology.
03
Measurement and Verification (M&V)
Validating savings from energy efficiency and conservation projects.
04
Financial Analysis and Modelling
Supporting informed decision-making and project financing.
05
Investment Grade Audit (IGA)
Preparing audits to investment-grade standard.
06
IoT and Data Analytics
Emerging tools and technologies in energy auditing.
07
Renewable Energy Applications
Renewable integration within audit scope.
08
Transport Energy Efficiency
Internal combustion engines and electric vehicles.
