01The mandate

Republic Act 11285 madeefficiency a legal duty.Most organisations stilltreat it as a line item.

The Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act obliges designated establishments to appoint an energy manager, run conservation programs, and report annually. Compliance and cost reduction are the same project.

01
Appoint an energy manager
Designated establishments must have a qualified energy manager on record with the Department of Energy. Our CEM program is the DOE-recognised route to that qualification.
02
Run a conservation program
An Energy Efficiency and Conservation Program has to exist, be documented, and be measurable — which means it starts with an audit, not an intention.
03
Report annually
Annual consumption reporting to the DOE is mandatory. A monitored Energy Management System produces that report as a by-product rather than a fire drill.
04
Prove the savings
Measurement and verification separates a claimed saving from a banked one. Our auditors are trained to the standard that regulators and financiers both accept.
