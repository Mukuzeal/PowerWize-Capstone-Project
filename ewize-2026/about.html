EnergyWize Solutions Inc. (E-Wize Solutions) is a Department of Energy (DOE)-Recognized Training Institution (RTI) for Certified Energy Manager (CEM), Certified Energy Auditor (CEA), Authorized Energy Auditor (AEA), and Government Energy Management Program (GEMP) training courses. We also provide recertification training for Energy Managers and Energy Auditors to support the renewal of their accreditation with the DOE.

This official recognition enables EnergyWize to deliver DOE-certified training programs fully aligned with Republic Act No. 11285, the Energy Efficiency and Conservation (EE&C) Act. As an RTI we are dedicated to raising the standards of energy management across industries by ensuring that professionals are equipped with the technical competence, regulatory knowledge, and practical tools to implement energy efficiency and sustainability initiatives successfully.

Our training curriculum includes comprehensive modules on energy audits, energy management systems (EnMS), compliance with DOE requirements, and the integration of energy-saving technologies. These programs build capacity among energy professionals, enabling them to lead projects that reduce consumption, enhance operational efficiency, and contribute to environmental stewardship.

Beyond training, EnergyWize Solutions Inc. is a DOE-Registered Energy Service Company (ESCO). Through this accreditation we provide a complete suite of energy services.

Services under ESCO registration
