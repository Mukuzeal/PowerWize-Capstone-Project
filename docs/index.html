Full Title
POWERWIZE: AI-Enhanced Training and Consultation System with Blockchain Payment Verification and Tokenized Certificates for EnergyWize Solutions Inc.
01Overview

What the system does

EnergyWize Solutions Inc. runs accredited energy-management training programmes — Certified Energy Manager, Certified Energy Auditor, the Government Energy Management Programme for local government units and other government entities, plus renewals — and separately sells solar photovoltaic installations.

Both sides of that business ran on paper forms, spreadsheets, manually issued certificates, and consultations that depended entirely on an engineer being free to take the call. PowerWize replaces all of it with one system.

Three design commitments distinguish it from a conventional learning-management system, and each one is enforced in code rather than by policy.

Payments and certificates are anchored to a public blockchain. Each settled payment and each issued certificate is hashed and written to Polygon. A QR code resolves to a public page that recomputes the hash and compares it against the chain, so a tampered record is detectable by an employer with no access to the system. The write is deliberately best-effort — a failed anchor never invalidates a paid enrolment or an earned certificate.

The solar consultant cannot invent numbers. Every figure quoted comes from a deterministic sizing engine, never from the language model. A post-generation check discards the model's text entirely if it contains a number matching no computed value.

Identity is verified before an account can exist. There is no public signup route — an account is created only by redeeming a token issued after an administrator reviewed a government ID and a selfie face-matched against it.

02Project Context

What was actually broken

EnergyWize Solutions Inc. is a DOE-accredited Recognized Training Institution. Delivering consistent training, validating payments and issuing authentic certificates is therefore a regulatory compliance matter, not merely an operational efficiency concern. Yet these processes ran on email and manual steps.

Training material, quizzes and certificates were distributed by email without any integrated or automated system. Because each action — submitting a quiz, confirming a payment, issuing a certificate — was performed by hand, staff time went to repetitive work and the process carried a standing risk of information loss, delay and error. Given that the CEM, CEA and GEMP programmes are DOE-accredited, those errors carry regulatory consequences.

Rather than rest on general descriptions such as "time-consuming" or "inefficient," the study documented each concern against the evidence that established it, gathered through preliminary interviews and process observation with EnergyWize staff.

Beyond these, the existing setup had no automated tool for Solar PV consultation computation — system sizing, load estimation, panel configuration and cost estimation all depended on manual calculation by staff, limiting both consistency and the volume of clients the company could serve professionally.

Payment verification used no cryptographic or blockchain safeguards, leaving enrolment-fee records disputable, and certificates issued under the CEM, CEA and GEMP programmes carried no tamper-evident validation. As employers and government agencies increasingly expect verifiable digital credentials, the absence of a secure tokenized certificate system is a competitive liability and a compliance risk.

All of these point to one technical hurdle: the lack of a single integrated digital platform that applies AI to CEM, CEA and GEMP training and evaluation, automates Solar PV consultation computation, secures payments through blockchain verification, and delivers tamper-proof tokenized certificates. That gap is what PowerWize was built to close.

03Objectives

What the system set out to achieve

General objective — to design, develop, and evaluate PowerWize — an AI-Enhanced Training and Consultation System with Blockchain Payment Verification and Tokenized Certificates — to improve the operational efficiency, data integrity, and service quality of EnergyWize Solutions Inc. in managing its DOE-accredited energy certification training programs, specifically the Certified Energy Manager (CEM), Certified Energy Auditor (CEA), and Government Energy Management Program (GEMP) Training and Workshop, as well as its Solar PV consultation services.

Specific objectives
  1. To develop an AI-supported learning assistant for the CEM, CEA, and GEMP online training programs by:
    • Analysing trainee performance and learning patterns to generate contextual explanations of complex course materials
    • Providing intelligent quiz hints and personalized feedback based on individual knowledge gaps
    • Guiding trainees through technical concept comprehension and program evaluation
    • Assisting instructors in creating quiz questions aligned with learning objectives
  2. To develop an AI-assisted Solar PV consultation computation engine that:
    • Analyzes client-supplied data, including energy load requirements, site location, available roof or land area, and equipment specifications
    • Generates Solar PV system sizing recommendations based on predefined engineering parameters and client-supplied data
    • Reduces the need for manual computation by automating predefined Solar PV sizing and estimation procedures
  3. To implement a blockchain-based payment verification module that:
    • Records and validates payment transactions for CEM, CEA, and GEMP enrolment fees and Solar PV consultation fees
    • Produces a blockchain-anchored, tamper-evident payment audit trail
  4. To design and deploy a tokenized digital certificate issuance system that:
    • Generates, issues, and securely stores blockchain-anchored certificates for participants who complete CEM, CEA, or GEMP training assessments
    • Enables third-party verification of issued certificates
    • Provides blockchain-based verification mechanisms intended to improve certificate integrity and traceability
  5. To build an administrative dashboard that provides EnergyWize Solutions Inc. personnel with tools for:
    • Managing user accounts and access levels
    • Monitoring training completion and progress across all three programs
    • Reviewing Solar PV consultation requests
    • Generating operational reports for data-driven decision-making
  6. To evaluate the system's functional performance, usability, and overall quality through functional testing and User Acceptance Testing (UAT), focusing on:
    • Functional suitability
    • Performance efficiency
    • Interaction capability
    • Reliability
    • Security
    • Maintainability

These evaluations are guided by selected quality characteristics of the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 software quality standard and the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), administered to administrators, trainers, and CEM/CEA/GEMP training participants of EnergyWize Solutions Inc. Solar PV consultation clients use the consultation module but are not surveyed as part of UAT.

04Methodology

How it was built and evaluated

The study's research design is Developmental Research, with a descriptive-evaluative approach for assessing usability and effectiveness. Agile is the software development methodology used to build the system iteratively — not the research design. The manuscript keeps the two separate deliberately, so that a way of building is not mistaken for a way of knowing.

Seven Agile development phases
01Requirement & Analysis

Stakeholder interviews with EnergyWize management and staff; definition of system scope, user roles and functional requirements; technology stack selection; sprint planning and product backlog creation.

02Design

System architecture; database schema and entity-relationship modelling; UI/UX wireframing for all modules; blockchain smart-contract schema; AI integration specification; design review with stakeholders.

03Implementation

Sprint-based coding of all modules — AI learning assistant, Solar PV computation engine, blockchain payment verification, tokenized certificate issuance, administrative dashboard — with biweekly sprint reviews.

04Testing

Unit and integration testing; performance testing of the blockchain layer; security testing of payment and certificate modules; validation of PV outputs against engineering benchmarks; Black-Box Testing by 10 IT professionals.

05Development

Iterative refinement from testing findings; resolution of defects and performance issues; re-development and re-testing of affected modules before reintegration; sprint backlog updates.

06Maintenance

Post-deployment monitoring; fixes and optimisation from UAT findings; updates to AI models and smart contracts; preparation of final system documentation, user manuals and the deployment package.

07Launch

Formal deployment; final User Acceptance Testing with all 48 respondents; structured feedback via the UAT questionnaire and System Usability Scale; final revisions and system handover.

Who evaluated it. Total enumeration of 48 EnergyWize stakeholders — one super user, one administrator, one secretary, 20 trainers and 25 trainees — plus 10 IT professionals selected by purposive sampling as system testers, each with at least two years of professional experience.

How it was measured. Black-Box Testing on a dichotomous pass/fail scale, and a 25-item UAT questionnaire on a five-point Likert scale drawn from selected ISO/IEC 25010:2023 characteristics and Technology Acceptance Model constructs, with the ten-item System Usability Scale as a supplementary measure.

05System Features

Six functional areas

Each area below corresponds to a section of the screenshot gallery that follows.

01Administration

Administrative dashboard

The gate into the whole system — nothing proceeds until registrations are worked.

  • Registration review with document inspection, approve and reject
  • Document images purged after review; only a digest is retained
  • Account management with per-account trainer capability grants
  • Training schedule and batch management
  • Analytics, audit log, and Excel exports across five entities
  • Three staff tiers: Admin, Super User, Secretary
02Instruction

Instructor training management

A trainer console for authoring, assessing, and tracking.

  • Module authoring with file attachments and publication control
  • Quiz builder across four question types
  • AI quiz generation drafted from module material
  • Practical exams with evaluation criteria and optional camera
  • Grading queue flagging what the auto-grader could not settle
  • Per-trainee progress including recorded quiz tab switches
03Learning

Trainee training & AI feedback

A syllabus resolved from the approved programme, not from identity.

  • Materials with recorded reading progress
  • Timed quizzes; automatic grading for three of four question types
  • In-module AI assistant answering questions on the material
  • Practical exam submission and returned written feedback
  • Progress computed identically for trainee and trainer views
  • Per-module rating collection
04Consultation

Solar PV consultation

Retrieval-grounded advice where no figure is written by the model.

  • Structured site survey with electricity-bill OCR
  • Deterministic engine: sizing, cost range, savings, payback, yield
  • Solara answers in English and Taglish, citing its sources
  • Off-topic questions refused before any chat model is called
  • Model text discarded if it contains an uncomputed number
  • Engineer review stored alongside computed figures, never over them
05Payments

Payment interface & verification

Card, QRPH, corporate invoicing, and an on-chain receipt.

  • PayMongo card and QRPH checkout for individual enrolment
  • Corporate invoicing from an approved roster, with audited overrides
  • Database-level idempotency keys — a retried webhook cannot double-charge
  • Unmatched webhooks quarantined raw, never dropped
  • Discount codes with expiry, usage cap, and programme scoping
  • Public receipt verification against the blockchain record
06Certification

Tokenized certificate & verification

A certificate an employer can authenticate without asking anyone.

  • Issuance gated on completion of every module in the programme
  • Certificate hash anchored to Polygon at issue
  • Public QR verification showing transaction, block, and confirmations
  • Hash recomputed and compared, so tampering is detectable
  • Expiry tracking where the programme defines one
  • Re-anchor action for certificates issued while offline

07Technology

Stack and implementation

BackendPython 3 · Flask · gunicorn — 13 blueprints
DatabasePostgreSQL 18 · raw psycopg2, no ORM, 36 tables
SchemaApplied idempotently in code at import time — no migration files
TemplatingJinja2 with shared macros
FrontendVanilla JavaScript — no framework, no build step
PaymentsPayMongo — card, QRPH, hosted checkout
BlockchainPolygon Amoy testnet via web3.py
AIGroq Cloud or local LM Studio, selected per workload
RetrievalCosine similarity over PostgreSQL REAL[] embeddings
VisionID classification, selfie face-match, electricity-bill OCR

Three independent provider settings govern AI use — one for text generation, one for vision, one for embeddings. The split is deliberate: a model good at reading a government ID is not the one you want writing quiz questions, and changing the chat model must never force a re-index of the retrieval corpus.
