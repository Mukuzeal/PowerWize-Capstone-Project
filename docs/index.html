Full Title
POWERWIZE: AI-Enhanced Training and Consultation System with Blockchain Payment Verification and Tokenized Certificates for EnergyWize Solutions Inc.
01Overview

What the system does

EnergyWize Solutions Inc. runs accredited energy-management training programmes — Certified Energy Manager, Certified Energy Auditor, the Government Energy Management Programme for local government units and other government entities, plus renewals — and separately sells solar photovoltaic installations.

Both sides of that business ran on paper forms, spreadsheets, manually issued certificates, and consultations that depended entirely on an engineer being free to take the call. PowerWize replaces all of it with one system.

Three design commitments distinguish it from a conventional learning-management system, and each one is enforced in code rather than by policy.

Payments and certificates are anchored to a public blockchain. Each settled payment and each issued certificate is hashed and written to Polygon. A QR code resolves to a public page that recomputes the hash and compares it against the chain, so a tampered record is detectable by an employer with no access to the system. The write is deliberately best-effort — a failed anchor never invalidates a paid enrolment or an earned certificate.

The solar consultant cannot invent numbers. Every figure quoted comes from a deterministic sizing engine, never from the language model. A post-generation check discards the model's text entirely if it contains a number matching no computed value.

Identity is verified before an account can exist. There is no public signup route — an account is created only by redeeming a token issued after an administrator reviewed a government ID and a selfie face-matched against it.

02Problem Statement

What was actually broken

EnergyWize's training and consultation operations were constrained by manual, paper-dependent processes with no verifiable record of what had been issued.

Enrolment could not be trusted or traced. Registrations arrived as paper forms and email attachments. Verifying that an applicant was who they claimed meant manually comparing a photocopied ID against a submitted photograph, with no record of who checked what, or when. Nothing prevented an unverified enrolment.

Certificates could not be authenticated. A printed certificate is only as credible as the organisation vouching for it. An employer or accrediting body holding one had no way to confirm it was genuine and unaltered without contacting EnergyWize directly — and EnergyWize had no way to prove a record had not been modified after issue.

Payments were reconciled by hand. Corporate enrolments covering dozens of employees were tracked on spreadsheets, with no systematic link between a settled invoice and the specific trainees it entitled, and no durable proof of settlement.

Training delivery did not scale. Assessment was manual at every stage. Writing quizzes consumed instructor hours that could have gone to instruction, and a trainee's progress existed only in whatever record their instructor happened to keep.

Solar consultations were a bottleneck. Every technical question — even a basic one about sizing, net metering, or battery necessity — required an engineer. Prospects waited for callbacks, and interest decayed while they waited. Deploying a general-purpose chatbot instead was not an acceptable substitute: an assistant that confidently invents a system size or a payback period does measurable commercial damage.

Nothing was auditable. Across all of the above there was no single record of who approved an enrolment, who altered a price, who issued a certificate, or on what basis a consultation was quoted.

03Objectives

What the system set out to achieve

General objective — to design and develop an AI-enhanced training and consultation system with blockchain payment verification and tokenized certificates for EnergyWize Solutions Inc., addressing the manual processes, unverifiable records, and consultation bottlenecks identified above.

Specific objectives
  1. Digitise and secure enrolment end to end Replace paper registration with online programme-specific forms, screen uploaded government IDs and selfies with a vision model at the point of upload, and make account creation strictly invitation-only so no unverified trainee can enter the system.
  2. Anchor payment records to a public blockchain Hash each settled payment and write it to the Polygon network, exposing a public verification page so that proof of settlement does not depend on EnergyWize's own records remaining intact.
  3. Issue tokenized, independently verifiable certificates Anchor each issued certificate on-chain and attach a QR code resolving to a public page that recomputes and compares the hash, allowing any third party to detect tampering without access to the system.
  4. Automate assessment and reduce instructor load Provide quiz generation from module material, automatic grading for multiple choice, identification, and enumeration, and an in-module AI assistant answering trainee questions — while routing essay answers to human review.
  5. Deliver reliable solar PV consultation without an engineer present Ground every answer in EnergyWize's own documentation through retrieval, compute every quoted figure with a deterministic sizing engine, and discard any model-authored text containing a number that does not match a computed value.
  6. Support corporate enrolment as a first-class case Let a company coordinator submit an employee roster without an account, bill one invoice across the cohort with database-level idempotency, and gate cohort access through classes whose entitlement is re-derived on every visit.
  7. Make every consequential action auditable Record approvals, account changes, price overrides, exports, and certificate issuance against the staff member responsible, and surface the record through an audit log and exportable reports.
  8. Evaluate the system against a recognised quality standard Assess functional suitability, usability, reliability, security, and the remaining ISO/IEC 25010 characteristics through user acceptance testing with the partner organisation's staff and trainees.
04Methodology

How it was built and evaluated

Developmental research, delivered iteratively. Each cycle put a working slice of the system in front of EnergyWize staff and folded their response into the next one, rather than specifying the whole system once and building to that specification.

P1Analysis

Requirements and process capture

Interviews and document review with EnergyWize staff to map the existing enrolment, training, certification, billing, and consultation workflows — including the paper forms the public registration pages had to reproduce faithfully.

P2Design

Architecture and data modelling

Data-flow diagrams, use-case models, and the entity-relationship design. Permission design was consolidated into a single capability map so that an interface control and the route behind it could not disagree.

P3Development

Iterative implementation

Flask and PostgreSQL, built blueprint by blueprint with the partner reviewing each increment. External integrations — payments, blockchain, AI providers — were isolated so that a failure in any of them degrades rather than blocks.

P4Testing

Verification and grounding checks

Functional testing per role, plus purpose-built scripts guarding the sizing mathematics, the retrieval threshold, and the check that stops the language model authoring numbers. A tracing harness exposes each consultation turn stage by stage.

P5Evaluation

ISO/IEC 25010 acceptance

User acceptance testing with the partner organisation's administrators, trainers, and trainees, evaluated against ISO/IEC 25010 product-quality characteristics.

05System Features

Six functional areas

Each area below corresponds to a section of the screenshot gallery that follows.

01Administration

Administrative dashboard

The gate into the whole system — nothing proceeds until registrations are worked.

  • Registration review with document inspection, approve and reject
  • Document images purged after review; only a digest is retained
  • Account management with per-account trainer capability grants
  • Training schedule and batch management
  • Analytics, audit log, and Excel exports across five entities
  • Three staff tiers: Admin, Super User, Secretary
02Instruction

Instructor training management

A trainer console for authoring, assessing, and tracking.

  • Module authoring with file attachments and publication control
  • Quiz builder across four question types
  • AI quiz generation drafted from module material
  • Practical exams with evaluation criteria and optional camera
  • Grading queue flagging what the auto-grader could not settle
  • Per-trainee progress including recorded quiz tab switches
03Learning

Trainee training & AI feedback

A syllabus resolved from the approved programme, not from identity.

  • Materials with recorded reading progress
  • Timed quizzes; automatic grading for three of four question types
  • In-module AI assistant answering questions on the material
  • Practical exam submission and returned written feedback
  • Progress computed identically for trainee and trainer views
  • Per-module rating collection
04Consultation

Solar PV consultation

Retrieval-grounded advice where no figure is written by the model.

  • Structured site survey with electricity-bill OCR
  • Deterministic engine: sizing, cost range, savings, payback, yield
  • Solara answers in English and Taglish, citing its sources
  • Off-topic questions refused before any chat model is called
  • Model text discarded if it contains an uncomputed number
  • Engineer review stored alongside computed figures, never over them
05Payments

Payment interface & verification

Card, QRPH, corporate invoicing, and an on-chain receipt.

  • PayMongo card and QRPH checkout for individual enrolment
  • Corporate invoicing from an approved roster, with audited overrides
  • Database-level idempotency keys — a retried webhook cannot double-charge
  • Unmatched webhooks quarantined raw, never dropped
  • Discount codes with expiry, usage cap, and programme scoping
  • Public receipt verification against the blockchain record
06Certification

Tokenized certificate & verification

A certificate an employer can authenticate without asking anyone.

  • Issuance gated on completion of every module in the programme
  • Certificate hash anchored to Polygon at issue
  • Public QR verification showing transaction, block, and confirmations
  • Hash recomputed and compared, so tampering is detectable
  • Expiry tracking where the programme defines one
  • Re-anchor action for certificates issued while offline

07Technology

Stack and implementation

BackendPython 3 · Flask · gunicorn — 13 blueprints
DatabasePostgreSQL 18 · raw psycopg2, no ORM, 36 tables
SchemaApplied idempotently in code at import time — no migration files
TemplatingJinja2 with shared macros
FrontendVanilla JavaScript — no framework, no build step
PaymentsPayMongo — card, QRPH, hosted checkout
BlockchainPolygon Amoy testnet via web3.py
AIGroq Cloud or local LM Studio, selected per workload
RetrievalCosine similarity over PostgreSQL REAL[] embeddings
VisionID classification, selfie face-match, electricity-bill OCR

Three independent provider settings govern AI use — one for text generation, one for vision, one for embeddings. The split is deliberate: a model good at reading a government ID is not the one you want writing quiz questions, and changing the chat model must never force a re-index of the retrieval corpus.
