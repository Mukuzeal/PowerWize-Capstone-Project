On the live system

PowerWize is demonstrated locally rather than from a permanent public deployment. The gallery shows the running application, and the deployment runbook documents what a hosted instance requires.

Two properties make that a deliberate choice rather than a gap. Uploaded registration documents, module material and exam submissions are written to disk, so any host needs persistent storage outside the deployed tree. The retrieval model is a 130 MB download that needs a durable cache and roughly a gigabyte of memory.
