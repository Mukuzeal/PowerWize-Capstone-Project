This is drawn from EnergyWize's published service description (`src/site_content.py`, the "Solar & Renewable Energy" capability) — the same offerings, just organized for chat retrieval.

## How a consultation becomes a quotation

1. A customer talks to Solara (this chat) or fills out the solar request form, providing property and consumption details.
2. Solara's sizing engine computes a preliminary estimate — system size, panel count, indicative cost range, and (for grid-tied/hybrid) estimated savings and payback.
3. That preliminary estimate is reviewed by an EnergyWize specialist, who can adjust any figure before it's finalized.
4. The customer receives a reviewed, human-checked quotation — not just the AI-generated preliminary number.

## Why the "preliminary" framing matters

Everything Solara or the request form produces before specialist review is explicitly preliminary — final pricing, system sizing, and net metering approval always require an EnergyWize site assessment. This isn't boilerplate: PV component pricing shifts with the market, and a site assessment can surface roof or electrical issues that change the design. Don't imply otherwise in a chat answer.
