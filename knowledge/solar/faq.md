# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I run solar for just my aircon (or one appliance)?

Yes, EnergyWize can build a dedicated system for a single appliance, and that's a reasonable request. The general recommendation is a whole-property system instead — see `grid-tied-vs-off-grid-vs-hybrid.md` for why (mostly: cost per usable watt, and surplus going to waste). The genuine exception is when the appliance is physically far from the main service and running power back is impractical.

## What happens at night, or on a cloudy/rainy day?

For a plain grid-tied system: it draws from the grid as normal, same as before solar was installed — there's no interruption. For a hybrid system with battery storage: stored daytime surplus covers some or all of that gap, depending on battery size and usage. For an off-grid system: the battery bank is specifically sized to cover at least a day with no sun, since there's no grid to fall back on.

## Do I need a permit or utility approval?

Grid-tied and hybrid systems generally need coordination with the local distribution utility, particularly for net metering (see `net-metering-philippines.md`). EnergyWize supports this process as part of installation, but approval timing is the utility's decision, not a fixed EnergyWize commitment.

## Is my roof suitable?

Depends on available area relative to the system size needed (see `system-sizing.md`), plus condition, orientation, and shading — which is why a site assessment happens before anything is finalized, even if a preliminary estimate already flagged the roof as feasible, limited, or insufficient based on area alone.

## How long does installation take?

Not a number to state — see `installation-and-maintenance.md`. Timelines vary by system size and site conditions and the existing chat rules already treat an invented installation timeline as a hard no.

## I already have a small system — can it be expanded?

Generally possible but needs its own site assessment; whether the existing inverter and wiring can support additional panels without replacement depends on the original design, not something to answer generically in chat.

## Sulit ba ang solar sa bahay? (Is solar worth it for my home?)

Generally yes for most homes with a consistent daytime electricity bill, since the panels offset grid consumption for as long as the system lasts — but "worth it" depends on actual consumption, roof space, and how much usage happens during daylight hours versus at night. See `system-sizing.md` for what actually drives the computed savings and payback for a specific property; this is a conceptual answer, not a promised number — the real figure always comes from the CALCULATIONS block.

## Mataas ang kuryente namin, makakatulong ba ang solar? (Our electricity bill is high, will solar help?)

Solar tends to make the most financial sense for customers with higher monthly electricity bills. When a bill is very low, the monthly savings are small compared to the upfront cost of the system, so it takes much longer to pay back the investment.

## Worth it ba ang solar para sa maliit na bahay? (Is solar worth it for a small house?)

A smaller property generally means a smaller, cheaper system rather than a poor fit on its own — the relevant constraint is roof area relative to the system size the household's consumption calls for (see `system-sizing.md`'s feasible/limited/unfeasible categories), not property size by itself. A small house with a correspondingly small bill can still get a worthwhile, appropriately-sized system.
